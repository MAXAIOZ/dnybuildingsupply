About DNY Building Supply

A dedicated building materials and supply chain project

DNY Building Supply focuses on the building materials and construction supply sector, with Australia as a key market.

The project is developed under DNY Cross Border Group's broader cross-border trade and supply chain direction. It supports businesses that need to present products, connect with buyers, organize product information, coordinate sourcing needs, and communicate with trade partners in a more professional and efficient way.

DNY Building Supply can support both Australian-side market needs and international supply-side resources.

The project is suitable for companies involved in building materials, home improvement products, construction supply, project sourcing, fit-out materials, hardware, interior products, and related supply chain services.
