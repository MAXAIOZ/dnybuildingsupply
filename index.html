About DNY Building Supply

A dedicated building materials and supply chain project

DNY Building Supply focuses on the building materials and construction supply sector, with Australia as a key market.

The project is developed under DNY Cross Border Group’s broader cross-border trade and supply chain direction. It supports businesses that need to present products, connect with buyers, organize product information, coordinate sourcing needs, and communicate with trade partners in a more professional and efficient way.

DNY Building Supply can support both Australian-side market needs and international supply-side resources.

The project is suitable for companies involved in building materials, home improvement products, construction supply, project sourcing, fit-out materials, hardware, interior products, and related supply chain services.

How It Works

A practical workflow for product and market connection

Understand Requirements

We first understand the supplier, buyer or project team’s needs, including product category, target market, project type, buyer profile, sourcing requirements, catalogue needs and cooperation goals.

Organize Product and Business Information

We help organize product information, supplier background, specifications, images, catalogues, project cases and communication materials.

Prepare Market-facing Materials

We support product presentation, catalogue structure, English descriptions, inquiry materials, project case summaries and buyer communication content.

Connect with Relevant Opportunities

Where suitable, DNY Building Supply may support connection with builders, trade partners, suppliers, project teams, distributors or business contacts.

Support Follow-up Communication

We can help organize follow-up communication, buyer questions, supplier responses, quotation discussion, product clarification and next-step coordination.
